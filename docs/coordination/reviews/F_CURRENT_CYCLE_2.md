## Concrete current defects

1. **P1 — A triggered protective stop disappears when execution capacity is zero.** `src/backtest/engine.py:90-93` calculates capped exit quantity and returns before creating/updating the exit order when quantity is zero. `_protect` has already observed a stop/target/liquidation trigger, but neither trigger nor pending residual is retained. On the following bar a recovered price can leave the position open forever, despite the prior stop crossing. Current FORMULAS says exit residuals persist; this zero-fill residual does not. Executed explicit synthetic DSL probe: signal at bar 0 close; market entry one unit at bar 1 open 100 consumes that bar's one-unit capacity; low 90 crosses stop 95 with no remaining capacity; bar 2 opens 100, low 99. Result contains only the entry order/fill, no pending exit or closed trade, and an open one-unit position. A zero-liquidity stop must not be fabricated as filled, but its triggered exit order must persist to the next eligible liquidity evidence. Add the fixture above plus zero-capacity liquidation/target and already-open zero-capacity stop tests.

2. **P1 — Whole-bar future liquidity controls fills stamped at bar open without an availability cutoff.** `engine.py:158-161` loads a quantity bound for the entire current bar before `_enter`/open protective execution, then `_fill` (`46-49`) labels the resulting fill `modeled_open` at `start_ms`. `quality.py:77-79` accepts liquidity with only quantity and nonempty evidence_refs: no available_at, observation range, temporal scope, source kind, quality or point-in-time version. README describes this as verified finer evidence **for that bar**, not evidence known at its open. Executed explicit fixture: changing only that future bar's liquidity quantity from 0.5 to 1 changes the quantity filled at the same open timestamp from 0.5 to 1. This is future-dependent sizing/fill allocation represented as earlier precise execution, and the capacity envelope cannot express or reject evidence arriving later. Separate liquidity evidence available at open from intrabar realization; enforce cutoff/version/quality and consume evidence in temporal order, or represent fills dependent on whole-bar capacity with bar uncertainty rather than open precision. Add future-liquidity mutation/prefix, unavailable/partial/late liquidity and intrabar timing fixtures. OHLC simulation may deliberately use later range evidence for uncertain bar fills; that does not justify stamping future capacity decisions as known-open fills.

Neither finding executes or guesses a natural-language strategy. Both use the repository's explicit synthetic JSON DSL fixture and pure offline engine.
